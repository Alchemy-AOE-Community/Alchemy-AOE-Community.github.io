information: |
    - Duel (1v1)
    - Unique Round Robin System
    - Two Independently-Scored Sprints with 4 Rounds Each. Each Round Play-All-Two. 
    - Sprints 1 & 2 with 33% and 67% of Total Prize Pool, respectively.
    - Players Matched Against Nearest Available Opponents, Minimizing Skill Gap.
    - This season featured the debut of "Round Wild", allowing any number of participants.
    - Player Winnings Calculated From Amalgamation of Skill, Performance, Participation, etc.
    - Standard Victory Conditions 
    - Custom Map Pool Theme: Greece
    - This season used an old scoring system (before win rate), whereby ties and unplayed sets were recorded as 1-1 rather than 0-0.
    - PSRMs had not been "invented" yet, thus a triple-digit GSRM took its place.
    - No Established Map Supply Base Yet: Most scripts were sourced from TechChariot. They were developmental artifacts of the cancelled "Return of Rising Eagle Cup", which would have been hosted by CBassJr713. QFilip was the only creator to bid on the final map slot.
    - No Competitivity Evaluation: The tournament host accepted a similarity argument between ALS3 maps and standard maps, though they were not interchangeable as other online sources may suggest. 
    - No Civilization Draft: After selecting the second map, each player globally banned two civilizations. Then, civilizations were free-pick, no repeat within the same set.
