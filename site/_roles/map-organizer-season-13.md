## Background

The Alchemy AOE Community is a free association of random map scripters, tournament hosts, content creators, and skilled players dedicated to advancing custom random maps in Age of Empires II: Definitive Edition. We publish quality standards for new custom random maps and curate finished products in thematic competitions where players exhibit creative strategies for maximum entertainment value.

Our flagship competition, Alchemy League, has visited Greece, Space, and other historical and fantastical settings. For nearly three years, we've pushed the boundaries of custom map creativity and told stories of medieval conquest through innovative competitive environments. As our founding leadership takes on new professional commitments, we're looking to expand our volunteer leadership and bring fresh perspectives into the organization.

## Why Become Map Organizer?

This is an opportunity to take ownership of one of the most creative aspects of competitive Age of Empires II. You'll work directly with map scripters to help shape the competitive experience of an established tournament and eventually build considerable influence over the themes, settings, and strategic possibilities explored by future seasons of Alchemy League.

You don't need to be an expert random map scripter to succeed. Organizational ability, technical curiosity, initiative, and a willingness to collaborate are just as important.

After an initial training season, the Map Organizer will enjoy considerable autonomy in deciding where Alchemy League goes next and what maps are played on. If you've always wanted to see something happen in the AoE2 universe, this is your opportunity to help make it a reality.

## Role Description

The Alchemy AOE Community is seeking a volunteer Map Organizer, starting with the thirteenth season of our league. As the focal point for map procurement activities, the Map Organizer is responsible for designing the league map pool, and balances novelty and thematic immersion with traditional competitive Age of Empires II gameplay.

Using established [Alchemy AOE Community procedures](https://alchemy-aoe-community.github.io/RedirectLatest.html?file=CHEM-STND-001) and design standards (such as [STND-002](https://alchemy-aoe-community.github.io/RedirectLatest.html?file=CHEM-STND-002) and [STND-003](https://alchemy-aoe-community.github.io/RedirectLatest.html?file=CHEM-STND-003)), the Map Organizer:

- Recruits map creators.
- Drafts requests for proposals from the random map scripting community, describing the theme of the custom map pool ([example](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1QyAQtOuQCn5cpJ3ZFCfPAWcc3uw_K7ypq0DoI4rEMtU/edit?gid=430100805#gid=430100805)).
- Reviews map proposals, fitting different locations and styles into a complete pool with diverse but thematically appropriate offerings.
- Prepares specifications ([example](https://alchemy-aoe-community.github.io/RedirectLatest.html?file=CHEM-CSRM-FIW-01)) for random custom maps that fully define the needs of the competition (thematic appropriateness, readability, strategic variety, etc.) while still leaving freedom for random map creators to excel. Collects consensus and approvals on designs from creators and other internal and external stakeholders.
- Appoints a team and/or personally reviews random custom maps against their applicable specifications, identifying deviations from requirements and helping coordinate corrective action.
- Tracks milestones of map project completion and provides support or solutions for projects that may be falling behind schedule.
- Independently engages with Alchemy AOE Community resources to accomplish any of the above responsibilities.

Depending on experience, the Map Organizer may execute these responsibilities under varying levels of guidance from the Alchemy League tournament host. The position operates with delegated authority from the tournament host, who retains overall responsibility for league operations and final approval of the seasonal map pool.

The expected output is a complete, themed map pool containing at least 8 new maps for each season of Alchemy League. We currently hold two seasons per year, representing an ongoing development program of at least 16 new maps annually.

## Your First Season: Return to Alaska

The previous section describes the Map Organizer's long-term responsibilities. However, we recognize that taking ownership of an established map development program can involve a considerable learning curve.

For this reason, the successful candidate's first assignment will be a **Return to Alaska** in ALS13 (second half of 2027), revisiting a theme originally featured in ALS4.

We already have [substantial preparatory materials](https://alchemy-aoe-community.github.io/RedirectLatest.html?file=CHEM-PSRM-AK-1&utm_source=chatgpt.com), and nearly the minimum required map scripts. This allows us to provide a learning ramp with lower risk/pressure. If it goes well, the Map Organizer will take on a more independent role planning and directing future seasonal map packs.

## Time Commitment

Depending on experience and personal initiative ("what you want to do with it"), this role is estimated to require an average of 2–4 hours per week.

The initial training and map development period will likely span 3–5 months during the first half of 2027. Workload will fluctuate throughout the season, with occasional increases around proposal deadlines, map reviews, and tournament preparation.

The Map Organizer appointment is **permanent rather than seasonal**. The initial Alaska assignment is simply the first season under the new organizer, with the expectation that they will continue directing map development for subsequent seasons. The appointment may continue indefinitely, subject to the individual's continued interest and participation.

## Stipend

The Alchemy AOE Community is led by servant leaders and volunteers. Like other AoE2 Communities, we simply cannot afford to pay people in a way that resembles real-life employment or even fair compensation. However, a modest stipend is available:

- **Monthly Stipend:** $80–$120 USDC.
- **Determination:** The stipend amount will be established before work begins, based on the candidate's knowledge and experience.
- **Payment Frequency:** Monthly.
- **Payment Method:** Disbursed from the USA via the [Solana blockchain](https://alchemy-aoe-community.github.io/RedirectLatest.html?file=CHEM-MTHD-010).
- **Geographical Availability:** Payments redeemable through major exchanges in NATO member states and most neutral trading partners. Check availability before applying!
- **Taxes:** Any applicable tax obligations are the responsibility of the recipient.

For reference, serving in the position for 12 months would represent total stipend payments of $960–$1,440 USDC.

## Other Benefits

- **Engineering and Project Management Experience:** The Alchemy AOE Community's approach to design, procurement, and quality assurance draws heavily on real-world engineering practices. Knowledge gained by leading the development of custom AoE2 maps is highly transferable to other disciplines. An appreciation for specifications, compliance matrices, milestone reviews, and structured development processes separates fledgling creators from experienced designers.
- **Community Recognition:** Alchemy's themed map packs are a benchmark for AoE2 custom random map development, and other groups use our work as a reference. Association with these projects offers an opportunity to build a reputation and demonstrate technical expertise within the AoE2 community.
- **Tournament Context:**
  - Seasonal prize pools: $350 (typical).
  - Seasonal map budgets: $380 (typical).
  - Historical player skill range: 1k–2.5k 1v1 ranked DE ladder.
  - Approximately 30–40 dedicated players, playing eight rounds over three months.
- **Creative Autonomy:** After the first training season, Alchemy's Map Organizer has considerable influence over where our league goes and what maps are played on. You will have an opportunity to turn your own ideas into competitive tournament experiences within the league's established parameters.

"If you've always wanted to see it happen in the AoE2 universe, this is your chance to make it a reality."

## Qualifications

At a minimum, applicants for the Map Organizer role must:

- Be a member of the [Alchemy AOE Discord server](https://discord.gg/h9DvzHBNPr) from the application date (or earlier) until the end of 2026.
- Have a free [GitHub account](https://github.com/signup) for signing documents, previewing maps, etc.
- Exemplify Alchemy AOE Community values: cheerfully volunteer as a leader without expecting financial reward, and believe that clear planning and well-defined requirements can prevent many of the problems that arise in large, diverse organizations.
- Be a self-starter with the initiative and proactivity to anticipate technical challenges. Demonstrate eagerness to learn about custom random map making and tournament organization. Be unafraid to solicit help from other Alchemy AOE volunteers.
- Communicate effectively in English and maintain reasonable responsiveness to correspondence.
- Have a vision for where the Alchemy AOE Community will go in future seasons of our league.

## Desired Qualities

It would be an advantage for any applicant to this role if they also had:

- Experience organizing or administering AoE2 tournaments.
- Experience developing random custom maps, particularly those intended for competitive AoE2 settings.
- Technical writing experience.
- Any kind of engineering experience.
- Knowledge of skilled AoE2 gameplay and an ability to anticipate how maps will influence competitive gameplay and strategic decision-making.
- A time zone close to the East US.

## Teamwork

Any number of people may assist the Map Organizer with the responsibilities listed here. 

However, only one person will be recognized by the Alchemy AOE Community as filling this role. The stipend is paid only to the recognized Map Organizer; any arrangement to share it is handled privately between collaborators.
